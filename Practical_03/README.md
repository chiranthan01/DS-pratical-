# Practical 03: Exploratory Data Analysis

**Dataset:** Datasets/TeachingRatings.csv (463 rows, 13 columns)

**Aim:** Explore tenure, age and evaluation scores of instructors.

## Steps

1. Load the data and check its structure.
2. Compare tenure between minority and non-minority instructors.
3. Find mean and standard deviation of age for tenured and untenured instructors.
4. Plot a histogram of age.
5. Draw horizontal and vertical bar charts of gender counts.
6. Find the median evaluation score of tenured instructors.

## Results

- Tenured: 84.38% of minority and 76.94% of non-minority instructors.
- Age of tenured instructors: mean 47.85, standard deviation 10.42.
- Age of untenured instructors: mean 50.19, standard deviation 6.95.
- Gender counts: 268 male and 195 female.
- Median evaluation score of tenured instructors is 4.0.

## How to run

Open the notebook in the same folder with Jupyter Notebook or Google Colab and run all cells. The data is read from the Datasets folder one level above.

Required libraries: pandas, numpy, matplotlib, scipy.
